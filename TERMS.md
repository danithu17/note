## 1. Description of Service

ScribbleNote is a hybrid lecture notebook and visual canvas platform designed for university students, educators, and professionals. Features include:
- Local-first offline typed notes and freehand digital handwriting.
- Slide deck (PDF/PPTX) background import and vector annotation.
- Audio-synced lecture recordings and seekable playback.
- SM-2 spaced repetition flashcards.
- AI study tools including note summaries, quizzes, and semantic inquiry.
